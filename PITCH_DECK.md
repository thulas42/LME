# CreditEdge - Pitch Deck
## LMA Edge Hackathon Submission

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Loan market participants across Europe, Middle East, and Africa face critical challenges:

- **Fragmented Information**: Market data scattered across multiple platforms
- **Manual Processes**: Time-consuming workflows in origination, documentation, and trading
- **Limited Transparency**: Difficulty accessing real-time market insights
- **Inefficient Operations**: Delayed deal execution and missed opportunities

---

## 💡 Solution: CreditEdge

**A unified desktop platform that reimagines loan markets through:**

### 1. **Digital Loan Origination**
- AI-powered credit assessment
- Automated workflow management
- Real-time risk scoring
- 40% faster processing times

### 2. **Intelligent Document Management**
- LMA-compliant template library
- AI-generated document creation
- Automated compliance checking
- Version control and tracking

### 3. **Transparent Loan Trading**
- Real-time marketplace
- Price discovery tools
- Secondary market analytics
- Enhanced market transparency

### 4. **Sustainability & Green Lending**
- ESG scoring and assessment
- Green loan portfolio tracking
- Carbon impact measurement
- SDG alignment monitoring
